Privacy

This website is made to know as little about you as possible.

Who is responsible

Falkenberg Digital GmbH, Switzerland. You reach us at info@recoflo.com.

What this website does not do

It sets no cookies. It uses no analytics and no tracking. It loads no fonts, scripts or pictures from other providers, and it has no forms.

What happens when you open a page

The website is delivered by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. To deliver a page, GitHub processes technical data that your browser sends with every request, among them your IP address, and may keep it in its logs. We have no access to these logs.

When you write to us

If you send us an e-mail, we use what you write and your address to answer you and to prepare an offer if you ask for one. We do not pass it on, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the European General Data Protection Regulation, you may ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. An e-mail to the address above is enough.
